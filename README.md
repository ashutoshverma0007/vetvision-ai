\# VetVision AI



AI-assisted veterinary health screening and animal health management platform.



\## Vision



VetVision AI aims to assist animal owners and veterinarians by combining AI-powered image analysis with animal health records, longitudinal tracking, and veterinary consultation.



\## Current Status



🚧 Project under active development.



\## Planned Features



\- Animal profile management

\- Veterinary health records

\- Vaccination history

\- Medical history

\- Image-based health screening

\- AI-assisted disease/severity analysis

\- Scan history and longitudinal tracking

\- Veterinary consultation

\- Mobile-friendly interface



\## Research Direction



Initial research focuses on image-based screening and severity classification for Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD), with particular attention to the trade-offs between:



\- Model accuracy

\- Inference latency

\- Model size

\- On-device deployment
